tCam Viewer 2 connects to a tCamMini or tCam (https://github.com/danjulio/tCam) open-source thermal imaging camera over Wi-Fi, decodes its raw radiometric data, applies color palettes, and renders a live thermal video feed.

Features:

* Live radiometric thermal video streamed directly from the camera over a network connection, with a spotmeter, max/min temperature overlays, a live color histogram, and a color bar scale
* 10 selectable color palettes (Arctic, Banded, Blackhot, DoubleRainbow, Fusion, Gray, Ironblack, Isotherm, Rainbow, Sepia)
* Capture single frames, record continuous video, or run a scheduled time lapse
* Graph temperature points over time
* Library screen to browse, sort, and delete saved images, videos, and time lapses, grouped by date
* Full-screen image and video viewers with color bar, temperature labels, and spotmeter overlay
* Share or export images and videos to the device gallery
* Celsius or Fahrenheit units, manual or automatic gain control, adjustable emissivity and gain mode

This app only talks to a tCam camera on your local Wi-Fi network — it needs no account and collects no data about you beyond an automated crash report if it hits an unexpected error, sent so the developer can fix bugs (see the privacy policy for details).
